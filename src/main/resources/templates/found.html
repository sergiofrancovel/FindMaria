Celebrating Maria's Return

Maria Gomez Perez, a 12-year-old girl who went missing on May 29th, has been found safe. We thank everyone for their tireless efforts and support. Let's come together to celebrate her safe return.

If the family requests help for Maria, we will post the links to do that here.

Community Support

The community's support has been overwhelming. People shared Maria's story, participated in search efforts, and provided emotional support to her family. We couldn't have done it without you.
